The original template's Bulma stylesheet, page stylesheet, and Font Awesome
script are included unchanged. Vendor license
notices are retained in those files. The page's content is replaced with W-JEPA
paper content; the teaser uses the supplied architecture panels, and the
interactive DSADS figure is embedded in the page. The paper figure carousels
and their unused assets are omitted from this website folder.
Page interactions and W-JEPA content-specific CSS are adapted. The original
attribution and license links remain visible in the footer.
